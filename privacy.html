Privacy Policy

Effective Date:  November 5, 2026 Last Updated:  November 5, 2026

Fork in Nigeria ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how our mobile ordering application collects, uses, and safeguards customer data for direct pickup orders.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the details necessary to create your account, process orders, and communicate pickup readiness:

  • Contact & Account Verification: Your mobile phone number (used for passwordless SMS authentication) and your name.
  • Order History & Details: Items selected, customized modifiers, order totals, and fulfillment timestamps.
  • Payment Data: When paying by credit/debit card or Apple Pay, your payment information is collected and processed securely by our certified third-party payment gateway. We never store full card numbers or sensitive verification codes on our servers. If you choose to pay in person, payment is handled directly at pickup.
  • Device & Technical Data: Device operating system version, app version, and push notification tokens required to deliver order progress alerts.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use customer data strictly to operate our direct pickup service:

  • Authenticating your account via SMS verification codes.
  • Preparing and fulfilling your food orders accurately.
  • Managing payment authorization holds, charging completed transactions, or releasing uncaptured authorization holds.
  • Sending real-time transaction updates, including order confirmation, estimated pickup times, and readiness notifications via push alerts or SMS.
  • Assisting with customer support requests and facilitating direct reordering.

3. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared exclusively with necessary service providers:

  • Payment Service Providers: To process transactions, holds, and captures securely.
  • Telecommunications & SMS Gateways: To deliver authentication passcodes and order update alerts to your phone.
  • Infrastructure Providers: Secure cloud hosting and database systems that manage application functionality.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by applicable laws, legal process, or to prevent fraudulent activity.

4. Data Security & Retention

  • Security: We apply technical and administrative safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Payment information is handled through tokenized payment channels meeting industry data security standards.
  • Retention: We keep your profile and order history on file so you can review past receipts and place repeat orders. Unfulfilled transaction authorizations are voided automatically. You may contact us at any time to request data deletion.

5. Your Preferences & Controls

  • Push Alerts: You can enable or disable push notifications at any time through your device settings.
  • Account Updates: You may contact us to update your information, request copies of stored data, or request account removal.

6. Contact Us

For questions regarding this policy or to submit a data request, please reach out to:

Fork in Nigeria Email: support@forkinnigeria.com
